nature.com | 2026-05-01

The researchers generate squeezed, trisqueezed, and quadsqueezed states by combining two spin-dependent linear bosonic interactions. This method allows for the creation of non-Gaussian states, which are essential for continuous-variable quantum computation.

Why it matters: This work opens up new possibilities for quantum simulation and computation by providing a technique to generate high-order non-linear interactions that were previously inaccessible. The demonstrated quadsqueezing is over 100 times faster than conventional methods.

A symmetry-reconfigurable photodiode for sensing and computing

nature.com | 2026-04-30

Matched interests: AI, image processing

The paper introduces a photodiode with reconfigurable symmetry for in-sensor sensing and computing. This device uses silver bismuth sulfide and can be programmed to generate non-volatile, bipolar weights for direct photocurrent computation. The authors demonstrate its application in infrared transmissive imaging, eye image recognition, and actuator control.

Why it matters: This innovation addresses the power constraints of edge AI applications by enabling computation directly at the sensor level. This could lead to more efficient and powerful microrobots, wearable electronics, and other smart devices.
